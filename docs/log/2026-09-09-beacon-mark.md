# The working indicator is the logo now

The electron shell (2026-09-08) is retired after one day. The mark is **the
beacon** — a steady nucleus, two faint shells and one orbiting signal — which
is not a new drawing. It is `src/components/PhosphorLoader.tsx`, the thing the
app has drawn beside "Working" all along, promoted to the identity.

## Why the loader and not one of the twelve

The [logo review](2026-09-09-logo-review.md) drew a round of twelve candidates
against the loader's grammar, on the reasoning that the loader survives at 20px
in a sidebar lane while the mark did not survive at 32px. Three of the twelve
worked. The best of them, `07 Drawn P`, worked by making the icon and the
spinner the same object.

At which point the round answers itself. If the strongest candidate is the one
that converges on the loader, the loader is the mark, and a thirteenth
letterform is beside the point. Every problem the review found is a
consequence of the identity and the running-state being two different things:

| Review finding                        | How this fixes it                                            |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Concept renders at 1.18:1 and 2px     | Contrast hierarchy inverted — bright parts carry the mark    |
| App icon and website are two drawings | One glyph, one file, a test that fails on drift              |
| Favicon a hand-kept copy, no guard    | Generated by `generate-icons.mjs`, asserted byte-identical   |
| No component in `src/` draws the mark | `PhosphorMark` / `PhosphorLockup`, shown in Settings → About |
| In-app monochrome variant owed ×3     | The in-app mark _is_ the canonical one; the tile is derived  |

## The one rule that carries it

**The faint parts are allowed to vanish.** Shells at `.35` and `.2`; the
nucleus, orbit and head at full strength. That is the inverse of the last two
identities, which put the concept in `.10` rings and a 64px caption and so
shipped something nobody ever saw below 48px. Verified at 16/32/48/64 off the
generated PNGs, not asserted from the SVG.

## Where the geometry lives

`BEACON` in `src/components/PhosphorMark.tsx`, in the loader's 32-unit
viewBox — radii 11/6/3, strokes 1.5/1, orbit 2.5 carrying a head of r 2.5.
`build/icon.svg` is that object times `ICON_SCALE` (28), chosen because every
value stays a whole number at 1024. Opacities are deliberately a separate
export: they are the one thing that does not scale.

Two placements it is NOT in: `src/styles/index.css` no longer sets
`stroke-width` on the shells (a CSS declaration beats a presentation attribute,
so leaving it there silently overrode the component), and the SVG holds no
number that `BEACON` does not.

## What changed on disk

- `build/icon.svg`, `build/icon-light.svg` — the new mark and its ember twin.
- All platform binaries regenerated (`icon.png`, `icons/`, `icon.icns`,
  `icon.ico`, `icon-dock.png`) plus `site/public/favicon.svg`, which
  `scripts/generate-icons.mjs` now writes instead of leaving to memory.
- `src/components/PhosphorMark.tsx` — new. Owns `BEACON`, `BeaconGlyph`,
  `PhosphorMark`, `PhosphorLockup`.
- `src/components/PhosphorLoader.tsx` — same public API, now renders
  `BeaconGlyph` rather than its own copy of the paths.
- `src/features/settings/tabs/AboutTab.tsx` — the lockup, which is the first
  time any Phosphor build has drawn its own mark in the UI.
- `src/components/PhosphorMark.test.ts` — the drift guard.
- The site needed no component change: the landing-page redesign that landed
  the same day (#244) had already dropped the bespoke `PhosphorMark.astro` in
  favour of `<img src="/favicon.svg">` in `site/src/layouts/Page.astro`, so the
  new mark arrives there via the generated favicon. The drift guard asserts
  that layout keeps pointing at the file instead of inlining an `<svg>`.
- `docs/style-guide.md` §Logo rewritten; breaking rules 7 and 8 replaced.
- `docs/brand-explorations.md` and `docs/img/brand/*.svg` (42 files) deleted.

## Known trade-offs

- **The mark no longer says "P", or "phosphorus", or "15".** It says orbit,
  nucleus and signal. The chemistry now lives only in the name and in the
  wordmark beside it. That is a real loss of literal meaning, traded for a mark
  that is legible at every size it is actually displayed at and identical to
  what the app already shows while it works.
- **Retiring an identity a day after adopting it** is not a good pattern. The
  reason it is right here is that the electron shell was never really seen:
  the release that carried it is the same one being replaced, and the review
  found its concept had never rendered in the first place.
- **The exploration archive is gone**, against the convention in the file that
  said to add rounds rather than delete them. Deliberate, on request, to stop
  carrying 42 dead SVGs; git history has them.
- The inner shell is invisible at 16px and the outer shell nearly so. Expected,
  documented, and the reason the nucleus and head are full strength.
