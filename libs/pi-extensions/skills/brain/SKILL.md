---
name: brain
description: Search Brain for approved knowledge from earlier lanes when answering what we know, entering unfamiliar work, revisiting decisions, or encountering unfamiliar people, systems and terms.
---

Call `brain_search` before answering “what do we know about…”, when starting work in an unfamiliar area, before decisions that may already be settled, and when a person, system or term is unfamiliar.

Use `brain_related` to see everything about one entity, by name or id.

Memories are approved notes from earlier lanes. They may be out of date. Another project's memory is context, not a rule. Check what is true now before relying on them.

Each memory shows the date its lane said it, and when it took effect if the lane named one. When memories disagree, trust the newer one. Old open questions and risks may already be settled.

Never copy recalled memories into a final answer as new knowledge.
